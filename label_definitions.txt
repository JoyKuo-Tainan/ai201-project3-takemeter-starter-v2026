grounded: The post makes a claim and supports it with something specific and checkable, such as a number, a first-hand test, a named source or a concrete detail from the poster's own work.
hot_take: The post makes a confident claim about AI and offers no support. It might be true, but it's asserted, not argued.
reaction: The post is a personal emotional response to something that just happened to the poster or near them. It's mostly feeling, not argument.
